Shipping & returns

Everything is printed fresh when you order — here's exactly what happens, and when.

The life of your order

  • 1You order.Confirmation email within minutes. Reply to it any time — a human reads it.
  • 2We print (3–5 business days).Made on demand means zero warehouse waste. You'll get a "it's printing" note.
  • 3It ships (tracking same day).US: typically 2–4 days transit. International: 7–14 days depending on customs mood.
  • 4You both judge the result.If the human or the dog disapproves — swaps below, no interrogation.

Swaps, returns, defects

Wrong size? One free swap.

Within 30 days, any reason — including "I guessed her size wrong." Where stock allows we ship the replacement first so nobody waits twice.

Print defect or transit damage?

Send a photo to our email = instant reprint. No need to ship the broken one back; gift it to a nemesis.

Just didn't love it?

Still talk to us. Unused items in original condition can come back within 30 days for a refund minus original shipping. We'd rather adjust than argue.

Where do you ship?

Currently US + international select. New regions open as fulfillment partners clear them — the guide list hears first.
