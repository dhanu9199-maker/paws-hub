The one mistake: buying "dog mom" gear for a breed-specific person. It reads like calling her partner "that guy who lives here." Specificity IS the gift — the breed name is where the love lives.

What NOT to buy

Paw-print generic anything (she has opinions), squeaky toys shaped like other dogs (traitorous), clothes FOR the dog without asking (doxies have wardrobe opinions too), and anything that says "world's best dog mom" — she knows, and the bar is higher than a mass-printed slogan.
