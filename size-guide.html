Size guide

Unisex cuts run true to shirt size. Gift-buying for someone else? Between sizes, size up — oversized is the look most breed moms prefer anyway.

The 60-second method (no body measuring)

  1. Lay their best-fitting tee flat. Measure armpit-to-armpit, then double it. That's the chest number.
  2. Match it on the chart below. If you land between two sizes — go up.
  3. Still unsure? Email us the measurement and we'll pick with you. A human answers.

S34–3627Snug unisex

L42–4429Relaxed fit

2XL48–5231Generous cut
Gift-buyers: sizing up is nearly always right. An oversized tee reads as cozy; a snug one reads as a mistake. And if the guess still misses, your first swap is free — any reason, including "I guessed."
