Two people, one dachshund, strong opinions about wiener-dog calendars.

Paws & Pastimes started the way most good gifts do: we couldn't find the thing. Every "dog mom" shirt said DOG MOM. Generic paw prints. Nothing that named her dog, her obsession, her particular brand of devotion.

So we draw our own. Breed-specific jokes for people whose phone gallery is 90% one animal. Craft jokes for people who buy yarn like others buy groceries. Plant and book jokes for the rest of the tribe.
