What dachshund moms actually buy

  • Breed-specific tees - "Doxie Mom" beats "Dog Mom" every time because it names HER dog. Our One & Only Doxie Mom shirt exists precisely for this reason.
  • Sweatshirts for dad too - the Certified Doxie Dad crewneck solves the harder person on the list.
